## This year's theme: online safety

Every 2026 project teaches something about staying safe online: spotting a phishing link, working out which downloads are safe, deciding what shouldn't go in a photo, building an encrypted handshake between two micro:bits.

## How the day runs

{{< steps >}}
{{< step number="1" title="10am: sign in and set up" >}}
Arrive at the hall, find a spot and get your laptop on the wifi.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step number="2" title="Pick a project" >}}
Seven to choose from, all listed below. New to this? Start with Scratch. The instructions come in a pile of other languages too, which helps if you'd rather read them at home first.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step number="3" title="Build it, then change it" >}}
Work through the instructions at your own pace, then make it yours: new sprites, extra levels, a harder version your mate can't finish, a completely different theme. Judges reward projects that go past the original.
{{< /step >}}
{{< step number="4" title="3pm: show and tell" >}}
Demo what you made, then pack up and head home.
{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

### Bring with you

- [ ] Scout uniform
- [ ] Laptop or Chromebook and charger
- [ ] Lunch and snacks, nut free please
- [ ] Water bottle, hat and enclosed shoes

Worth doing beforehand: [open a Scratch account](https://scratch.mit.edu/join) and try [a project from a past year](https://moonhack.com/previous-projects/). It makes the day much smoother.

## The seven projects

{{< feature-grid columns="4" >}}
{{< feature icon="eye" title="Priva-See Detective" headingLevel="h3" >}}
What's safe to put in a photo you share? Find what shouldn't be in shot. **Scratch, beginner.**
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature icon="shield" title="Cyber Safety Mission" headingLevel="h3" >}}
A quiz on safe choices online: names, addresses, meeting up in real life, and more. **Scratch, beginner to intermediate.**
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature icon="bug" title="Whack the Hack" headingLevel="h3" >}}
Whack-a-mole, except you only hit the safe downloads. Clones, variables and a timer. **Scratch, mid to advanced.**
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature icon="globe" title="Safe Site Sprint" headingLevel="h3" >}}
A platformer where you collect only the safe websites. Custom blocks and sensing. **Scratch, mid to advanced.**
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature icon="lock" title="Identity Guard" headingLevel="h3" >}}
Two micro:bits shake hands, encrypted, to prove who they are. **micro:bit, beginner.**
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature icon="search" title="Phishy Finder" headingLevel="h3" >}}
Read a URL closely enough and the phishy ones give themselves away. Lists, dictionaries, scoring. **Python.**
{{< /feature >}}
{{< feature icon="wand-magic-sparkles" title="AI: avatar or photo?" headingLevel="h3" >}}
Train a model to tell an avatar from a real photo, then work out why the avatar is the safer thing to post. **Teachable Machine, all levels.**
{{< /feature >}}
{{< /feature-grid >}}

## Prizes and submitting

We submit the day's projects as a group, so there's nothing for anyone to do individually. Australian entries go into the competition: four age categories, plus two judges' prizes on top. Judges score purpose, coding, presentation and engagement.
